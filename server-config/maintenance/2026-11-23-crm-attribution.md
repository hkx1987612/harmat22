# CRM attribution report (2026-11-23)

The manager-only report is linked from the existing inquiry summary. It uses the recorded date (Europe/Budapest), defaults to the last 14 complete days, and permits up to 31 complete days per query. Date/source filters are GET. Channel totals, raw inquiries and report-deduplicated qualified customers are separate. The existing quote, booking, sales actions and record tables are unmodified.

- YouTube V4 inquiry and Facebook use verified campaign tags. Google Search or V4 can also use a verified Google campaign ID. A bare click ID, a self-reported source or an unavailable archive is not proof. On a bounded read, the report can reconcile an exact saved GCLID to its first-party request and Google campaign ID before the inquiry. It does not use IP addresses, browser fingerprints, `gbraid`/`wbraid` as join keys, or send anything to Google. No click identifier or log content is emitted or stored by the report. An incomplete archive read leaves unproven sources unresolved.
- A qualified customer must have unambiguous identity and an existing sales-confirmed reservation-intent, reservation, contract or closed stage. A contact, pending appointment or inventory flag alone does not qualify. Explicit test/invalid records stay in raw totals, not qualified counts. Conflicting identities do not qualify; conflicting campaign evidence remains unresolved. Deduplication is in memory; no record is updated, merged or removed.
- Preflight reconciled the Nov 9–22 production cohort in a read-only transaction before installing anything. Candidate data and rendered ZH/HU totals matched the independent audit. Live Chrome verified the manager entry, total and channel counts, Search filtering, and the separate qualified view. Anonymous access was denied. Eight public route form/SEO contracts were unchanged; no forms were submitted. Isolated source, deduplication and archive fixtures, 148 native CRM UI checks, 121 contact-review checks, and 26 appointment integrity checks passed. No raw records, logs, authenticated screenshots, or customer identifiers are committed.
